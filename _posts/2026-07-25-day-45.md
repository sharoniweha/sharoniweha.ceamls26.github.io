  Today was Day 1 of the National Symposium on Equitable AI (NSEA), and it was an incredible experience. I got to attend some inspiring sessions featuring great speakers like Juan E. Gilbert, Hasavari Shirin, and Onare Mayowa. It was fascinating to hear Dr. Gilbert discuss his AI system that streamlines admissions by selecting strong applications, as well as its applications in U.S. voting systems. Another major highlight of the day was checking out an interactive humanoid demo. I learned about the work being done at Envision Code, where they teach young kids how to build and program cool tech. For instance, they showed off gloves that control toy cars through hand movements and heads with eyes that track facial features. Seeing that kind of hands-on innovation in action was super impressive and really cool to watch. I also spent time dropping into a few more sessions and browsing the poster presentations. It was awesome getting to see the poster work presented by my graduate mentor, Ricky, alongside other mentors, teachers, and speakers. Overall, it was a packed first day that left me feeling really inspired by all the unique projects and research being shared!
